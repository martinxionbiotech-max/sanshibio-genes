# Racing Pigeon Flight Gene Encyclopedia: 8 Flight Ability Genes at a Glance

> **Key takeaway**: This encyclopedia explains the **8 genes behind racing pigeon flight ability** (LDHA, DRD4, CRY1, MSTN, F-KER, LRP8, GSR and CASK) together with the science of selection. Whether you race sprints or long distances, you will find gene mechanisms, breeding strategies and testing services here in one place.

Welcome to the **Racing Pigeon Flight Gene Encyclopedia**. Compiled by [Hebei Sanshi Biotechnology Co., Ltd.](https://sanshibio.net/), it is built around the eight core markers used in [racing pigeon flight ability gene testing](https://sanshibio.net/flight-ability/) and explains each gene's molecular mechanism, testing significance and breeding application — helping fanciers move from "judging by experience" to data-driven selection.

## The eight flight ability genes at a glance

| Gene | Chinese name | English full name | What it indicates |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| LDHA | 乳酸脱氢酶 A | Lactate Dehydrogenase A | Endurance (lactate metabolism) |
| DRD4 | 多巴胺受体 D4 | Dopamine Receptor D4 | Homing determination |
| CRY1 | 隐花色素 1 | Cryptochrome 1 | Navigation and orientation |
| MSTN | 肌肉生长抑素 | Myostatin | Muscle power and burst |
| F-KER | 羽角蛋白 | Feather Keratin | Feather and wing quality |
| LRP8 | 低密度脂蛋白受体相关蛋白 8 | LDL Receptor Related Protein 8 | Learning and memory |
| GSR | 谷胱甘肽还原酶 | Glutathione Reductase | Orientation in rain and overcast weather |
| CASK | 钙/钙调素依赖性丝氨酸蛋白激酶 | Calcium/Calmodulin-Dependent Serine Protein Kinase | Cognition and trainability |

## FAQ

### Can gene testing predict race results?

Not directly. Testing evaluates genetic potential, while results are also affected by training, management, weather, disease and form on the day.

### How many genes are enough?

We recommend testing all eight. Flight ability is the combined effect of many genes, so testing only one or two gives an incomplete picture.

### Does sampling require a blood draw?

No. Pulling feathers with the follicle attached is enough. Squabs can be sampled and the bird is barely affected.

### What is the difference between a genetic ID card and a pedigree?

A pedigree can be altered, while a genetic ID card is based on DNA and cannot be forged. Only the two together make a lineage trustworthy.

### How long does a gene test take?

The automated sequencing workflow is efficient; the exact turnaround time is given on our [testing services](https://sanshibio.net/services/) page.

### Can squabs be tested?

Yes. Testing is possible at the squab stage, which makes it easy to identify potential early and manage lineages from the start.
